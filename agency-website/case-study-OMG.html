BEAUTY INDUSTRY 2026 CASE STUDY

OMG - Livestream Design System for Beauty E-Commerce

Redesigning Indonesia's foremost autism advocacy platform into an accessible, empathetic, and scalable digital ecosystem.

CLIENT OMG (Oh my Glam)
SERVICES Creative Design, Motion Graphics
TIMELINE 1 Weeks
DELIVERABLES E-Commerce Design
01 / THE CONTEXT

Powering a High-Energy Payday Sale for a Young Beauty Brand

OMG (Oh My Glam) is an Indonesian cosmetics and skincare brand by PT Paragon Technology and Innovation, offering high-quality, affordable makeup and skincare for confident, active young consumers. For its Payday Mega Sale, OMG needed a bold, fast-moving live streaming experience that could spotlight hero products — from its No. 1 lip cream to cushions, Two Way Cake, and SPF 50 tone-up — while riding the momentum of brand ambassador Sheila Dara to convert viewers into shoppers in real time.

With thousands of monthly visitors seeking urgent guidance, their legacy digital portal struggled with visual clutter, complex navigation paths, and a lack of sensory-friendly accessibility standards.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Designing a Livestream That Sells in Real Time

A

Bold, On-Brand Visuals

Building a striking deep-red key visual language with product bubbles, price callouts, and stage-curtain drama that felt premium yet still fun and accessible.

B

Clarity Under Pressure

Organizing dense sale information — bundles, tiered prices, and ambassador looks — into clean, scannable layouts that viewers could read instantly during a fast live session.

C

Motion That Converts

Creating motion design assets that drew attention to flash deals and hero products, driving urgency without cluttering the live stream interface.

03 / THE SOLUTION

A Cohesive, Compassionate Design System

We created key visual designs for OMG's live streaming sessions and developed motion design assets built around a deep-red, high-contrast identity — product bubbles, clear price tiers, and a Sheila Dara makeup-look banner that carried across every Payday Mega Sale variation. The modular layout let bundles, prices, and hero products swap quickly between sessions while keeping the look consistent and energetic.

04 / THE IMPACT

The campaign gave OMG's Payday Mega Sale a bold, cohesive livestream identity that made hero products and bundle deals impossible to miss — with motion assets and modular layouts that kept every session on-brand, fast to update, and built to drive real-time conversion.
