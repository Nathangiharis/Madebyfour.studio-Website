BEAUTY INDUSTRY 2026 CASE STUDY

Mad For Makeup: Redesigning & Crafting Shopify Web Development for a Beauty E-Commerce Brand

Redesigning Indonesia's foremost autism advocacy platform into an accessible, empathetic, and scalable digital ecosystem.

CLIENT Mad For Makeup
SERVICES Web Development, Shopify Development
TIMELINE 2 Months
DELIVERABLES Web Application, Shopify Store
01 / THE CONTEXT

Building an Inclusive Beauty Experience, End to End

Mad For Makeup is a revolutionary beauty brand that aims to make high-quality makeup accessible to everyone, regardless of their skin tone, face shape, or budget.

We designed the website concept and built the platform on Shopify — creating a clean, user-friendly shopping experience anchored by new features that let customers explore, personalize, and connect with the brand in ways a standard storefront couldn't.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Turning a Storefront into an Experience

A

Designing Net-New Features

Conceiving and visualizing features that didn't exist yet — Virtual Try-On, Membership, engraving, and shade selection — with interfaces intuitive enough to feel native to the shopping flow.

B

Clarity Across Many Pages

Unifying a growing set of pages — landing, website benefits, FAQ, product details, membership — under one clean, consistent visual system so nothing felt bolted-on.

C

Shopify Within Brand

Delivering these concepts on Shopify without compromising the brand's minimal, editorial aesthetic or the usability of the core purchase journey.

03 / THE SOLUTION

A Clean System Built Around New, Personal Features

I designed a cohesive website concept and built it on Shopify, centered on a minimal editorial layout with a consistent header, footer, and product grid. On top of the core store, I created visuals for a suite of new features:

- Virtual Try-On — an interactive interface letting shoppers preview makeup on their own face before buying.

- Membership — a dedicated tier and sign-up flow to reward loyal customers.

- Shade Selection on Product Details — a clear swatch grid so customers pick the right shade with confidence.

- Exclusive Online Services & Benefits — personalized gifts, special packs, and free shipping surfaced as reasons to shop online.

04 / THE IMPACT

A Website That Feels as Inclusive as the Brand The concept transformed a standard storefront into an experience-led platform — where Virtual Try-On, personalization, and membership give shoppers real reasons to explore and return. Built on Shopify with a modular, on-brand system, every new feature stays consistent, easy to update, and ready to scale as the brand grows.
