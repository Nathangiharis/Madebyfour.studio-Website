PRINTING INDUSTRY 2026 CASE STUDY

Okheelo Printing - Social Media Design

Giving a Local Print Shop a Scroll-Worthy Voice

CLIENT Okheelo Printing
SERVICES Social Media Design, Motion Graphics
TIMELINE Subscription-based
DELIVERABLES Social Media Design, Motion Graphics
01 / THE CONTEXT

Democratizing Access to Autism Education and Professional Therapy

Okhéelo Print (@okheeloprint) is a digital printing business in Tangerang, Indonesia, with branches in Modern Land and Gading Serpong, offering fast printing services — stickers, posters, banners, invitations, photos, and mugs.

To stand out in a crowded local market, Okhéelo needed more than a service list; it needed a personality. I handled social media design and motion graphics, developing its visual branding and a steady stream of creative assets that turned everyday printing into content people actually wanted to watch and share.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Making Printing Fun to Follow

A

A Memorable Brand Voice

Building a playful, recognizable visual identity — bold greens, a gold accent, and a friendly tone — that could make a printing business feel approachable and fun on the feed.

B

Consistency Across Content Types

Designing a system flexible enough to cover greetings, promos, educational "Printing 101" posts, and humorous reels while staying unmistakably Okhéelo.

C

Motion That Entertains

Producing motion graphics and reels that used humor to explain printing topics — CMYK vs. RGB, doff vs. glossy, digital vs. offset — without feeling like a dry tutorial.

03 / THE SOLUTION

A Playful, Consistent Social Content System

We developed Okhéelo Print's visual branding and designed a full library of social media assets around a bright green-and-gold identity, a consistent logo lockup, and a warm, humorous tone. The content spanned several recurring formats:

- Greetings & Occasions — holiday and national-day posts (Isra Mi'raj, Idul Adha, Hari Lahir Pancasila) that kept the brand present in daily life.

- Educational "Printing 101" — bite-sized explainers on CMYK/RGB, die cut vs. kiss cut, mockups, and print methods.

- Promo & How-To — clear, friendly posts for online ordering, invoice claims, and finish choices (doff vs. glossy).

- Humorous Reels & POV — relatable, motion-driven skits that gave the brand its distinct personality.

04 / THE IMPACT

A Print Shop That People Actually Follow The social media design and motion graphics gave Okhéelo Print a distinct, likeable identity that turned routine printing topics into shareable content. With a consistent visual system across greetings, education, promos, and reels, the brand built a feed that feels fun, trustworthy, and easy to keep growing.
