BEAUTY 2026 CASE STUDY

Emina E-Commerce Live Design

Crafting design needs for Emina E-Commerce Live.

CLIENT Emina Beauty
SERVICES Creative Design, Motion Graphics
TIMELINE 1 Weeks
DELIVERABLES E-Commerce Design
01 / THE CONTEXT

Bringing Playful Energy to a Year-End Beauty Launch

Emina is an Indonesian cosmetics and skincare brand under Paragon Technology and Innovation, loved by young consumers for its playful identity and affordable, everyday products.

For the End Year Wrap-Up Sale, Emina needed a campaign that could introduce its new Macaron Blur Powder while driving urgency across a crowded, high-competition holiday shopping season — all without losing the soft, youthful charm the brand is known for.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Turning a Product Launch into a Scroll-Stopping Campaign

A

Cohesive Visual Identity

Building a key visual language of soft macaron pastels, playful pedestals, and clean product hierarchy that felt fresh yet unmistakably Emina across every touchpoint.

B

Motion That Sells

Designing motion graphics for the Macaron Blur Powder launch that highlighted texture, shades, and bundle value in the first few seconds to hold a young, fast-scrolling audience.

C

Live-Ready Adaptability

Developing flexible visual design plans for Emina's live streaming campaigns, with modular layouts that swap products, prices, and promos on the fly without breaking the look.

03 / THE SOLUTION

A Sweet, Modular Campaign System

We created key visual designs and motion graphics for the Macaron Blur Powder launch and built a modular visual system for Emina's live streaming campaigns — pairing macaron-inspired pastels, playful 3D pedestals, and clear price callouts so bundles, hero products, and flash promos could be swapped in seconds while staying on-brand.

04 / THE IMPACT
Real Buzz for a Playful Year-End Launch The campaign gave the Macaron Blur Powder launch a distinct, shoppable identity across the End Year Wrap-Up Sale — with motion assets and a flexible live-stream toolkit that kept promos consistent, on-brand, and easy to scale across sessions and product bundles.
