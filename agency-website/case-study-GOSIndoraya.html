HEALTHCARE & WELLNESS 2026 CASE STUDY

Teman Autis — Being a Bridge Between Autism and Everyone Else

Redesigning Indonesia's foremost autism advocacy platform into an accessible, empathetic, and scalable digital ecosystem.

CLIENT Yayasan Teman Autis Indonesia
SERVICES UI/UX Design, Web Development, Brand System
TIMELINE 8 Weeks (Discovery to Deployment)
DELIVERABLES Web Application, Design Tokens, Resource Hub
01 / THE CONTEXT

Democratizing Access to Autism Education and Professional Therapy

Teman Autis is a pioneering non-profit dedicated to connecting families, clinical therapists, educators, and the broader community with verified information on autism spectrum support in Indonesia.

With thousands of monthly visitors seeking urgent guidance, their legacy digital portal struggled with visual clutter, complex navigation paths, and a lack of sensory-friendly accessibility standards.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Balancing Emotional Warmth with Rigorous Clinical Utility

A

Cognitive Accessibility

Creating sensory-conscious interfaces with calm color contrasts, clear typography hierarchy, and zero jarring animations for neurodivergent visitors.

B

Directory Architecture

Structuring an extensive national database of certified therapy clinics, workshops, and verified psychological experts with lightning-fast filters.

C

Mobile-First Readiness

Ensuring 100% of diagnostic guides, screening questionnaires, and article archives load instantly across varied mobile devices in sub-second speeds.

03 / THE SOLUTION

A Cohesive, Compassionate Design System

We developed a bespoke modular UI system centered on soft oceanic tones, clear iconography, and guided step-by-step diagnostic workflows that reduce cognitive fatigue for stressed parents.

Interactive Clinic & Specialist Directory Finder
Sensory-Conscious Mobile Consultation Booking
